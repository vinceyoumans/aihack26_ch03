MADE-UP DATA. NOBODY IN THESE FILES IS REAL.

3,000 synthetic people going through cancer treatment while wearing a Fitbit, made by the Crane Lab
(Sylvester Comprehensive Cancer Center) for the Miami AI Hackathon.

synthetic_patients.csv               one row per person
synthetic_daily.csv                  one row per person per day (465,466 rows)
synthetic_hourly_250_patients.csv    hour by hour for 250 of the people
synthetic_250_patients.json          the same 250 people, ready for a web or phone app

Every column is explained in ../README.md, section 3.
